HMK 635 WL

HMK 635 WL
HİDROMEK wheel loaders fully meet user expectations with its features such as high performance, versatile usage, comfort and safety for operator. It provides productive and efficient work in applications such as quarry, gravel, mining and concrete plant works, where they are frequently used. Heavy-duty loaders provide high breakout power and excellent control ability.

  • General Information

    HMK 635 WL fully meets the expectations of its users with the features such as high performance, versatility, operator comfort and safety. HMK 635 WL wheel loader is commonly used by quarry enterprises and concrete plants, for industrial applications and public services and in many fields with different attachments providing productive and efficient work possibilities. Heavy duty type loader HMK 635 WL offers high breakout power and excellent control capability in severe working areas.

  • Technical Specs

      • ENGINE
        • Brand - Model
          Mercedes-Benz MTU6R1000
        • Type
          High power and torque output, water cooled, turbocharged diesel engine.
        • Maximum Power
          282 HP (210 kW) @1800 rpm
        • Maximum Torque
          1200 Nm @ (1200 - 1600 rpm)
        • Displacement
          7.700 cc
        • Number of cylinders
          6 in line
        • Bore x Stroke
          110 x 135 mm
        • Emission Class
          Stage V
      • TRANSMISSION
        • Number of Gear
          5 forward, 3 reverse gears
      • DRIVING SPEED
        • 1st Gear
          5 km/h
        • 2nd Gear
          10 km/h
        • 3rd Gear
          16 km/h
        • 4th Gear
          25 km/h
        • 5th Gear
          40 km/h
      • LOADER PERFORMANCE
        • Bucket capacity
          3,5 m³
        • Maximum breakout force
          17650 kgf
        • Static tipping load
          15900 kgf
      • OPERATING WEIGHT
        • Operating Weight
          20.600 kg
      • HYDRAULIC SYSTEM
        • Type
          Closed center type hydraulic system with LS type variable displacement piston type pumps.
        • Pump Flow
          283 L/min @2100 rpm
        • Pressure
          300 bar

  • FAQ

    • When was HİDROMEK established?
      HİDROMEK was established in Ankara, Türkiye in 1978.
    • What products does HİDROMEK manufacture?
      HİDROMEK manufactures machines belonging to the product group of backhoe loaders, wheel, crawler and special purpose excavators, motor graders, soil compactors, wheel loaders and telehandlers.
    • Where are HİDROMEK's production facilities located?
      HİDROMEK manufactures at seven facilities: five in Ankara, one in Izmir, and one in Thailand.
